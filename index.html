  1. Book one

    Liberation

    Published September 5, 2023. 760 pages.

    Freedom. One might sacrifice almost anything to get it. Freedom from the bonds of society. Freedom from fear. Freedom from captivity.

    But freedom is not enough with darkness infecting the Middle Gate. A terrifying madness paints the streets with commoner's blood, enemies harry the borders, and those able must fight. Those gifted must find their magic and take heart, for the greatest battles are yet to come.

    The imprisoned must survive. The suffocated must breathe. The subdued must rise.

A world pulsing with magic, violence, and a spreading plague of madness

The Keepers of Midgate spans land and sea.

  • From dusty deserts of Fire
  • to the deepest ocean trenches,
  • from snowy mountains
  • and vast plains
  • to the glorious sky above.

What you'll find in my books

  • Multiple third-person POV
  • Parallel character-driven plot lines
  • Adult fantasy, not YA
  • Dragonriders
  • Medieval societies with race- and social rank-based conflicts
  • Tragic and mysterious backstories
  • Lost princes and conflicted princesses
  • Women with swords and magic
  • Cinnamon roll men and men who fall first/harder
  • Mysterious plots and a nebulous evil
  • Romance subplot, mostly fade-to-black (but not always)
